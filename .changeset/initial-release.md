Initial release: schema-driven Amino binary and JSON encoding/decoding, byte-compatible with go-amino, with `Any` support, well-known types, repr (`MarshalAmino`) types and `sortJSON` for tm2 sign bytes. The `@gnolang/amino-ts/gno` subpath ships generated schemas for all tm2 and gno.land amino types (transactions, messages, accounts, blocks, votes, validators, ABCI, events, genesis), with address, coin and sign-bytes helpers.
